# Customer Retention & Churn Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analysing customer retention and churn using the Telco Customer Churn dataset.

## Objective
The main objective is to identify customer groups with higher churn rates and understand the factors related to customer churn.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Key Findings
- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,174
- Overall Churn Rate: 26.54%
- Overall Retention Rate: 73.46%
- Month-to-month customers have a churn rate of 43%.
- Customers with 0–12 months of tenure have a churn rate of 47.44%.
- Electronic Check customers have a churn rate of 45.29%.
- Fiber optic customers have a churn rate of 41.89%.

## Recommendations
- Improve the experience of new customers during their first year.
- Encourage month-to-month customers to choose longer contracts.
- Investigate issues related to electronic check payments and fiber optic services.

## Files Included
- Customer_Churn_Analysis.xlsx – Excel analysis and dashboard
- Customer Retention & Churn Analysis.docx – Project report

## Conclusion
The analysis shows that contract type, customer tenure, payment method and internet service are important factors related to customer churn.
